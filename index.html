    Quick help

    To watch: Paste the link and press Play. The player will automatically find a working connection method.

    Stream not loading? Press "Refresh Stream". If it still doesn't work, the link may have expired — ask for a new one.

    Want something else? Press "Try Another Link" to paste a different link.

    On your phone? Use your phone's volume buttons. Rotate sideways for a bigger picture.
