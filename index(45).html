    Quick help

    To watch a stream: Paste the link into the box above and press Play.

    Stream not loading? Make sure "Use connection helper" is turned on (it's on by default). Then press "Refresh Stream". If it still doesn't work, the link may have expired — ask for a new one.

    Want to watch something else? Press "Try Another Link" to go back and paste a different link.

    On your phone? Use your phone's volume buttons to control the sound. Tap the video to show or hide controls. Rotate sideways for a bigger picture.

    Supported links: .m3u8 (HLS streams) and direct video links like .mp4.
