    Quick help

    To watch a stream: Paste the link into the box above and press Play.

    Stream not working? Press "Refresh Stream" to try reconnecting. If it still doesn't work, the link might have expired — ask for a new one.

    Want to watch something else? Press "Try Another Link" to go back and paste a different link.

    On your phone? Use your phone's volume buttons to control the sound. Tap the video to show or hide controls. Rotate your phone sideways for a bigger picture.

    Supported links: Works with .m3u8 streams and direct video links like .mp4 files.
